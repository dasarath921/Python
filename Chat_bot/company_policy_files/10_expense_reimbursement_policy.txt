COMPANY HR POLICY: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

1. Employees may claim eligible business expenses that were approved under company procedures.
2. Original bills or valid digital receipts must be submitted.
3. Expense claims should be submitted within 15 days of the expense.
4. Manager approval is required before reimbursement.
5. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
6. Claims must include the business purpose, date, amount, and supporting receipt.
7. Reimbursement is subject to verification and the applicable expense limits.
