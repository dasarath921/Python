COMPANY IT POLICY: INFORMATION SECURITY

1. Employees must never share their passwords.
2. Company systems may only be accessed using authorized accounts.
3. Strong, unique passwords must be used.
4. Multi-factor authentication must be enabled where required.
5. Suspicious emails, links, or attachments must be reported to the IT team.
6. Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
7. Company devices must be locked when unattended.
8. Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately.
9. Confidential data must only be stored in approved company locations.

Note: This is sample policy data for chatbot testing, not an actual company policy.
