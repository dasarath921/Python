COMPANY HR POLICY: ATTENDANCE

1. Employees must record attendance on every working day using the approved system.
2. Attendance errors should be reported to HR as soon as possible.
3. Employees must inform their manager about unexpected absences.
4. Three consecutive unauthorized absences may result in disciplinary action.
5. Employees should follow the attendance and shift rules communicated by their team.
6. Employees must not record attendance on behalf of another employee.

Note: This is sample policy data for chatbot testing, not an actual company policy.
