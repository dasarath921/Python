COMPANY HR POLICY: EMPLOYEE CONDUCT

1. Employees must behave professionally and respectfully.
2. Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and visitors with respect.
3. Harassment, bullying, and discrimination are prohibited.
4. Company confidential information must not be shared with unauthorized people.
5. Employees must disclose conflicts of interest through the appropriate company process.
6. Company property must be used responsibly.
7. Concerns about misconduct should be reported through the designated HR channel.

Note: This is sample policy data for chatbot testing, not an actual company policy.
