COMPANY HR POLICY: RESIGNATION

1. Employees are expected to provide 30 days' notice before resignation.
2. Resignation requests should be submitted to the reporting manager and HR.
3. Employees must complete the required knowledge transfer before their last working day.
4. Company assets must be returned before the employee's exit is completed.
5. Employees should complete exit formalities and submit required documents.
6. Final settlement is processed according to company procedures and applicable law.
7. Any exception to the notice period requires written approval from authorized management.
