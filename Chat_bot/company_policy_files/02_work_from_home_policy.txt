COMPANY HR POLICY: WORK FROM HOME

1. Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
2. Manager approval is required before working remotely.
3. Employees must be available during core hours from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
4. Employees must attend scheduled team meetings while working remotely.
5. Company laptops and approved systems must be used for official work.
6. Confidential company information must be protected when working remotely.
7. Employees should inform their manager if internet or power issues affect their work.

Note: This is sample policy data for chatbot testing, not an actual company policy.
