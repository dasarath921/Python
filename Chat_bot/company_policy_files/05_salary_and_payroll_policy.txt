COMPANY HR POLICY: SALARY AND PAYROLL

1. Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.
2. Salary slips are available through the employee portal.
3. Salary details must be treated as confidential.
4. Salary discrepancies should be reported to HR within 7 days of receiving the salary slip.
5. Statutory deductions and applicable taxes are handled according to relevant laws.
6. Employees should keep their bank and tax details up to date in the employee portal.
