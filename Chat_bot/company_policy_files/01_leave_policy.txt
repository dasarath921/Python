COMPANY HR POLICY: LEAVE POLICY

1. Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year.
2. Employees are entitled to 12 sick leaves per calendar year.
3. Employees are entitled to 18 earned leaves per calendar year.
4. Leave requests should be submitted at least 2 days in advance.
5. Emergency leave may be requested on the same day, subject to manager approval.
6. Unused earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.
7. Employees should check their available leave balance before applying.
8. Leave approval depends on business requirements and the manager's decision.
