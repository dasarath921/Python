COMPANY HR POLICY: HOLIDAYS

1. The company follows its published annual holiday calendar.
2. National and regional holidays are observed according to the applicable company calendar.
3. Employees should check the official calendar for holiday dates.
4. Working on a holiday requires prior manager approval, except in emergencies.
5. Compensatory leave may be provided for approved holiday work, subject to company rules.
6. Team-specific support schedules may apply during holidays.
