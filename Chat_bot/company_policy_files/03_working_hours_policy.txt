COMPANY HR POLICY: WORKING HOURS

1. Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
2. Employees are expected to complete 8 working hours per day.
3. The standard lunch break is 1 hour.
4. Employees should inform their manager if they expect to arrive late.
5. Overtime requires prior manager approval.
6. Team-specific schedules may apply where communicated by management.
7. Employees should follow their assigned shift timings.

Note: This is sample policy data for chatbot testing, not an actual company policy.
